# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

Responsive web app (Next.js) with a PWA manifest, service worker and offline page. Mobile web is a primary context; there is no native app.

## Users

Primary: people aged roughly 22–35 travelling solo or as a pair on a budget. They don't plan six months ahead; they decide on Thursday to leave for the weekend, are fine with hostels, and value authenticity over luxury. They usually already want to go — what they need is someone to sort out the flight and the plan in a few minutes.

Archetype (from BRAND.md): Marek, 27, remote developer in Wrocław, sees a clip of Porto on Thursday night, answers six questions, flies out Friday morning.

Secondary: families and groups of friends. The quiz supports them, but product, copy and content decisions are aimed at the solo/pair spontaneous weekend.

Other audiences: the admin (Kacper) using the usage and cost dashboard at `/admin`.

## Product Purpose

Włóczykij turns a short quiz (budget, dates, travel style, who's going) into a ready weekend trip: six destinations that fit the budget, real flight prices from Polish airports, and a day-by-day, hour-by-hour plan with prices, restaurants and budget tricks, generated by Gemini and streamed day by day.

Success: the visitor goes from "I want to go somewhere" to a bookable flight and a plan they trust in about three minutes, and then actually goes.

## Positioning

Not another flight aggregator and not a generic "travel planner". Włóczykij is the clever friend who always knows how to fly for pennies and what to do once you land. The quiz → real fares → editable streamed itinerary flow, priced in PLN from Polish airports, is the core mechanism.

"AI nie zastępuje przygody. AI ją odblokowuje." AI is the means, not the headline.

## Operating Context

- Entry paths: full quiz (`/quiz`), quick path (`/quick`), destinations browser (`/destinations`, `/odkryj`), flights (`/flights`).
- Plan view (`/plan/[tripId]`): streamed days, regenerate a single day, swap one activity, packing list, restaurant picks, Leaflet map, budget tracker, chat, PDF and `.ics` export, public share link (`/share/[tripId]`).
- Pre-trip reminder e-mail (Vercel Cron, daily 07:00), newsletter with double opt-in.
- Blog in MDX (`src/content/blog`) for SEO, focused on budget solo travel in Europe.
- Typical moment of use: on a phone, Thursday evening, deciding quickly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Monetisation: 2 free plans per month (credits); one-off pack of 5 plans for 14,99 zł; Pro unlimited at 19,99 zł/month or 149,99 zł/year (display prices live in `src/lib/prices.ts`); Stripe billing portal. No credits returns `402`.
- Auth via Supabase; rate limiting on expensive endpoints; flight results cached to stay within free-tier API quotas. Generation, flights and chat cost real money per call.
- Gemini output is NDJSON streamed per day and repaired (`validateAndFixPlan`); the UI must render days as they arrive rather than wait ~30 s.
- All UI and content are in Polish (`lang="pl"`, `pl_PL`); prices in PLN for flights/plans, local currency (often EUR) inside itineraries. Code and README in English.
- Stack: Next.js 16 App Router (breaking changes vs. older Next — read `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before writing code), React 19, Tailwind 4, Framer Motion, Zustand, Leaflet.
- Canonical domain: `wloczykij.me` (used in metadata, e-mails, SEO). BRAND.md's `wloczykij.app` is outdated on this point.

## Brand Commitments

Source of truth: [BRAND.md](BRAND.md).

- Name always written with Polish diacritics: **Włóczykij**. Reference to Tove Jansson's Snufkin (Polish: Włóczykij) is the spirit of the brand, but never a literal depiction of the copyrighted character.
- Tagline: "Obudź w sobie Włóczykija."
- Voice: casual but not sloppy, concrete not encyclopaedic, dry humour that's never forced, poetic in small doses. Talks like a friend over coffee.
- Voice is never: corporate ("Nasza platforma umożliwia…"), shouty ("NAJTANIEJ!!"), infantile (emoji and exclamation overload), apologetic ("Niestety nie udało się…"). Example: "Coś poszło nie tak. Spróbuj jeszcze raz — zwykle działa za drugim."
- Identity avoids the travel-startup clichés BRAND.md lists: airplane as main element, map pin, 2020 SaaS gradients, round "W" monogram.
- BRAND.md also records a logo direction and supporting palette; those are visual commitments owned by future design work, not restated here.

## Evidence on Hand

- The product is live, with real functionality: quiz, real flight prices, streamed plans, payments, e-mails, admin panel.
- Real content: five Polish blog articles in `src/content/blog`, a sample Barcelona itinerary on the homepage, logo at `public/logo.svg`, city photos via Unsplash API.

- README notes a missing screenshot/GIF of the quiz → plan flow.

## Product Principles

1. **Three minutes to going.** Every step should shorten the path from impulse to a flight and a plan; nothing gets in the way of Thursday-night decisiveness.
2. **The friend, not the platform.** Concrete, money-aware advice in a human voice beats feature lists and AI buzzwords.
3. **Budget is the brief, not a limitation.** Real prices, real costs per activity, and cheap tricks are the content people came for.
4. **Honest by default.** Show only real prices, real features and real evidence; no fabricated social proof.
5. **Respect the cost of every call.** Generation and flight searches cost money; UX should make each one count (stream, cache, edit a day instead of regenerating the trip).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific standard has been set. The app already ships a skip link ("Przejdź do treści") and light/dark theme colours; treat WCAG 2.1 AA as the working baseline until a stricter requirement is decided.
